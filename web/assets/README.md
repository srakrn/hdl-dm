# Sharing image

`sharing-image.png` is the 1200 × 630 Open Graph / Twitter image. `share-card.html` is its renderable source: open at 1200 × 630, wait for `document.fonts.status === 'loaded'`, then save a viewport PNG. It uses the site's cream (#f8f5ed), coral (#dc6555), and actual Playpen Sans Thai Bold font; the SIL OFL license is included.

The background was edited with the built-in imagegen tool from the user-supplied restaurant photo. The full edit prompt was:

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: the attached restaurant photo. Create a wide landscape 1.905:1 social-sharing background by tightly cropping ONLY the divided metal hotpot in the lower foreground of this exact photograph. Preserve its actual soup compartments, rims, handles and ladles and photographic detail; no recreation or new objects. Exclude all people and all upper restaurant background entirely. Convert the cropped photo to neutral black and white and reduce brightness substantially for legible light text to be overlaid later. Pot should fill the entire frame edge to edge with dark midtones and visible metallic detail. No text, no borders, no graphics. Output a clean dark grayscale photographic crop.

The exact Thai title is rendered as real text, not generated typography:

สูตรน้ำจิ้มไหตี่เลา
จากเอไอ
